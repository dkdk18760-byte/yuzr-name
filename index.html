Bukhara City

Bukhara is one of the oldest cities in Uzbekistan. It is famous for its ancient buildings, historical monuments, and rich culture. Bukhara was an important city on the Silk Road. Today, many tourists visit Bukhara to see its beautiful architecture, traditional markets, and UNESCO World Heritage sites.
